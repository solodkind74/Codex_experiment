          Battleship

          Hide five ships on a ten-by-ten grid, then hunt down the enemy fleet before it finds yours.

          1. Place your five ships on your grid. They can lie across or down, but not overlap.
          2. You and the computer take turns firing at one square of the other's grid.
          3. A red peg marks a hit and a white peg a miss. Hit every square of a ship to sink it.
          4. Sink the whole enemy fleet before it sinks yours.
